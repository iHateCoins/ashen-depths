# M03 / PR03 — Room-Based Encounters

## Gameplay shift

The three-wave training trial is replaced by a **populated, continuous dungeon**:

- Every chamber outside the entrance is populated when the expedition starts. Enemies are not teleported into the current room, and rooms never lock until cleared.
- Populations are deterministic for each dungeon seed. Near chambers generally contain pairs of Wretches; distant or cache chambers add a Brute; the Deep Chamber contains three Wretches and a stronger **300-HP Warden** (using the existing Brute behavior).
- Enemies retain their HP when the player leaves their room, and the existing A* pursuit can continue through hallways, limited by normal disengagement distance.
- Optional chambers are truly optional. The first completion goal is to **enter the Deep Chamber and defeat the Warden**. Killing other enemies is not required.
- The enemy objective HUD shows the next goal, enemies remaining overall, and surviving enemies originally assigned to the current chamber.
- A victory overlay now says **WARDEN DEFEATED**, with a touch-friendly **NEW EXPEDITION** button. The defeat overlay continues to say **RESTART EXPEDITION**.
- The procedural layout, fog, room discovery, minimap, Cinder Burst, touch movement, seeded URLs and Home Screen app updater all remain intact.

## Manual acceptance

1. After merging and deployment, open the installed iPhone app. Confirm version **M03 / PR03** and the new objective **Explore · Find the Deep Chamber**.
2. Move into a neighboring chamber. Its pre-existing enemies should react as you approach. Retrying or backtracking should not respawn enemies.
3. Retreat through a connected corridor: nearby enemies may chase, but your character never auto-chases them.
4. Visit the **Sealed Cache** as an optional branch and see its tougher group. You can retreat without clearing it.
5. Explore until you reach the **Deep Chamber**, then defeat the larger **WARDEN**. Once both conditions have occurred, the expedition is completed even if optional enemies remain.
6. Tap **NEW EXPEDITION** to restart into a fresh populated dungeon. Let enemies defeat you and confirm **RESTART EXPEDITION** also works.
7. Check minimap, fog, automatic updates, seeded layout reproduction and portrait/landscape touch controls.

## Future work

M03 / PR04 adds actual optional room objectives and special-room interactions, building toward loot in M04. Current enemy behavior deliberately reuses Wretch/Brute AI. The Warden's art and specialized mechanics will come later. The old wave-system modules and tests remain as historical prototype artifacts, but are not used in gameplay.
