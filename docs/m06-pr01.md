# M06 PR01 — Hybrid Affix Families and Item Tiers

## Pillar alignment
Item-driven character progression is the main reward loop. This update adds real equipment decisions rather than a new direct player-level power bonus. The Cache/Warden, existing Forge Ember rules and successful extraction remain unchanged.

## Expanded affixes
Four old legacy affixes remain valid: Sharp (Attack), Ember (Spell), Sturdy (Armor), Vital (Health). Four new hybrid modifier families combine offensive or defensive stats, using each affix's rolled magnitude:
- **Battlemage's Might**: full Attack Power plus half Spell Power.
- **Raider's Vigor**: full Attack Power plus half Maximum Health.
- **Bastion's Resolve**: full Maximum Health plus half Armor.
- **Spellward's Focus**: full Spell Power plus half Armor.

Dual-stat halves round to nearest whole (minimum 1 when the magnitude is nonzero); negative cursed affixes invert **both** contributions.

Slot-appropriate pools keep drop flavor: weapons have Sharp/Ember/Vital/Battlemage/Raider; armor has Sturdy/Vital/Ember/Bastion/Spellward; charms have Vital/Ember/Sharp/Battlemage/Spellward. Duplicate affix IDs cannot appear on the same newly rolled item.

## Deterministic item tiers
- New rolled affixes include `tier: 1 | 2 | 3`; old items without a tier remain valid and display without a tier.
- Maximum tier gated by item level: 1–7 => T1; 8–16 => T2; 17+ => T3 (future content).
- Warden rare drops remain guaranteed, level 8 and two affixes, but have a higher chance of tier 2 rolls. Common/magic/rare still roll 0/1/2 affixes, respectively.
- Roll magnitude scales with affix tier. Same seed/enemy still generates identical items, respecting the existing loot economy and item rarity conditions.

## UX and Forge compatibility
- Tooltip text in stash, inventory, and nearby loot shows the exact stat contributions of hybrid rolls and affix tier.
- Forge Ember cost, 25% erase/20% curse/55% reroll odds, one lifetime forge marker, next-run spend rule and atomic stash writes remain unchanged.
- Forge reroll selections include new hybrid IDs from the item's legal slot pool; resulting affixes respect level tier ceilings. Cursing hybrid affixes penalizes both stats, no free stat value at zero.
- Existing stash key/schema and saved gear/unlocks load unchanged, including older forged items with absent affix tiers. Malformed affix tiers are rejected instead of overwriting saves.

## Tests and review
- Unit: all hybrid stat projections, negative/zero values, deterministic rolls, pool legality, duplicate prevention, tier gates, legacy save migration and malformed tier rejection.
- Browser: desktop/mobile landscape/iPhone WebKit stash and tooltip reading with a mixture of tier-2 hybrid and no-tier legacy equipment.
- Regression: existing Forge Ember, extraction, cache/Warden, gear management, mobile skill loadout, and stash transaction suites.

### Out of scope
No new items/rarities above Rare, unique effects, enemy drop chance revisions, item crafting safety changes, or unlock catalog changes. Those are later M06 milestones.
