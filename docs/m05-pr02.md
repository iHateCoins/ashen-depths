# M05 / PR02 — Skill Supports and Combat Scaling

## Scope
Add one optional support socket per equipped active skill, using the existing two unique active skill slots. All supports are temporarily available for prototype testing at the entrance camp. They are NOT inventory items, loot drops, permanent unlocks, stash objects or new save fields.

## Support modifiers
| Support | Damage | Area radius | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Focused Power | +35% | -25% | unchanged |
| Expansive Blast | -20% | +50% | unchanged |
| Swift Casting | -20% | unchanged | -30% |

- Each equipped active skill takes **one** support or **none**.
- The **same support ID cannot be used by two different active skills** in the same loadout.
- Selection is **camp-only**, consistent with active-skill and secured-stash withdrawal rules. Skills and supports remain inspectable afterward.
- Supports affect **real hit damage, actual AoE and target clustering, and the interval until the next cast**. A support does not bypass line-of-sight checks, auto-targeting or the two-active-skill limit.
- Equipment's flat Spell bonus is added before support damage multiplication. Final damage and radius are rounded to whole numbers, minimum 1.
- Support assignment, replacement and removal leave consumed cooldowns intact. Removing a skill releases its support socket. New expedition resets both skills and supports.
- No new UI attacks or automatic player chasing, no persistence schema migration and no changes to loot ownership / extraction rules.

## Player acceptance (phone and desktop)
1. Open **SKILLS** at entrance camp and attach **Focused Power** to **Cinder Burst**. Confirm visible stats **47 damage** and **79px radius** (unmodified 35 and 105).
2. Equip Frost Bloom as second active skill. Its support menu must prohibit **Focused Power**, already assigned to Cinder. Equip **Swift Casting** instead.
3. Fight a clustered group: supported AoE should visibly change size and hits, not just text. Confirm cooldowns still auto-advance while playing.
4. Switch Cinder to **Expansive Blast** at camp: displayed 28 damage and 158px radius; wide AoE should hit nearby enemies outside normal Cinder radius, subject to walls.
5. Leave entrance camp. Reopen SKILLS: support dropdowns and skill equipment buttons are disabled until next expedition.
6. On defeat/extraction/restart: current support selections vanish, and Cinder Burst is the only equipped skill as before. Previously secured gear remains in the stash.
7. Test mobile portrait and landscape touch controls, finish/extraction buttons, Sealed Cache and Shrine after the Warden, and no accidental waystone channel on skills modal opening.
