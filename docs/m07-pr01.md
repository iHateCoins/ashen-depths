# M07 PR01 — Warden-Gated Depth Descent

## Goal
Establish the full expedition decision loop: **defeat the floor Warden, extract with your progress or descend for tougher encounters and higher item levels**. Character progression remains the main motivation; extraction supplies the tension.

## Rules
- Each expedition begins at depth 1 with the existing deterministic ?seed behavior. On defeating that floor's Warden, **FINISH EXPEDITION** remains available and **DESCEND TO DEPTH N · KEEP LOOT AT RISK** appears beside it in the mobile-safe interaction dock.
- No Warden kill = no descent. Opening the Cache, visiting waystones, clearing regular rooms or merely entering the deep chamber never unlocks the next floor.
- Descending loads another deterministic procedural floor with a Warden and Cache, but **does not write to localStorage**.
- Equipped gear and the entire carried bag, current HP and max-HP effects, chosen active skills/supports, and pending skill/Forge Ember rewards all remain with the player. Unpicked ground items and temporary cache combat boons are deliberately abandoned on descent.
- The entrance camp is only usable for withdrawals/forging/loadout edits when starting the expedition; it is **not** reopened by descending to another floor.
- Every floor has its own Warden and Cache discovery choices, independently claimable. Rewards remain unsecured until a successful extraction on any floor. Unselected discoveries can be carried forward and claimed later, then secured with the rest.
- Completing an extraction on depth N secures all bag/worn gear, all chosen skill unlocks and all selected Forge Embers in the existing **single atomic save**. Death, browser refresh, or a normal restart discards everything still at risk.
- The existing end-of-floor Warden Finish and waystone extraction interfaces are retained. No automatic floor transition and no automatic extraction.

## Initial scaling
- Depth 1 is unchanged: same world seed, enemy HP, strike damage, item levels and drops.
- New floor layouts and drops use a per-depth deterministic seed derived from the expedition seed.
- Per-depth enemies gain modest HP (+18% per additional floor, temporarily capped) and strike damage (+2 per depth, capped).
- Drops gain +2 item levels per depth, opening higher affix tiers/bases over time. A level-8 Warden rare becomes level 10 on depth 2.
- A provisional depth cap ensures item levels stay compatible with existing schema-1 stash validation. The intended endgame difficulty architecture remains future work.
