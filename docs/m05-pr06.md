# M05 / PR06 — Repeatable Forge Embers and Item Reforging

## Why this exists — Core pillars
Character and equipment progression is the primary motivation; boss survival and extraction are supporting mechanisms. Previously, after all eight skills/supports were discovered, Cache and Warden offered **no progression reward**. This update adds a repeatable, at-risk material that directly improves item customization instead of unlimited automatic character-level power.

## Gameplay
- Sealed Cache and Warden discoveries now always offer **Forge Ember** in addition to currently unknown active skills and supports. Even after the entire unlock catalog has been learned, both sources continue offering a Forge Ember.
- Each source offers at most **one choice per run**. Both Cache and Warden can yield one Ember each, and their order does not matter. New players may choose a skill unlock instead of currency.
- Forge Embers remain **at risk** until the player deliberately extracts through a waystone or finishes after defeating the Warden. Death, abandonment and reload lose unextracted Embers.
- Extracted Embers are added to the existing `ashen-depths-stash-v1` save alongside gear and skill unlocks **in one atomic storage write**. Existing saves without the optional `forgeEmbers` field continue to load as zero currency.
- At entrance camp, the **STASH** panel shows Forge Ember balance and a **REFORGE 1 EMBER** control beside each affix on stored Magic or Rare items. Pressing it randomizes only that specific affix's type and value within the legal affix pool for the item's slot and item level. The new roll may be better or worse. Non-selected affixes, item rarity, item level, base and item ID remain unchanged.
- Reforging costs one *secured* Ember, but only if the resulting item and new balance were successfully written in the **same atomic transaction**. Out-of-date tabs, quota errors and invalid selections are rejected without consuming currency or altering the stored item.
- Each item gets exactly ONE forge attempt for its lifetime, recorded on the item as an immutable-in-game forge marker even after withdrawal, re-extraction or browser reload. Previously acquired Embers are spendable at camp; newly extracted Embers cannot be used until a NEW expedition starts. Dungeon drops cannot be made safe by opening STASH.
- Currency is intentionally capped in the save validation at 999,999; random affix values scale with the item's original level, not with the number of times it was reforged.

## Acceptance on desktop / iPhone
1. With no unlocks, open Cache: choose either a skill/support discovery OR Forge Ember. It is pending until extraction.
2. With all eight unlocks, open Cache and/or defeat Warden: each source still offers exactly one Forge Ember choice.
3. Secure an Ember through extraction. On next expedition, STASH shows the new balance without losing earlier gear or skills.
4. At entrance camp, tap FORGE on one of two rare affixes. Verify an explicit confirmation warning explains 25% erasure, 20% curse, 55% reroll, and no second attempt. Confirm: exactly one Ember is spent; the selected affix is erased/cursed/rerolled and the permanently FORGED item survives reload.
5. Leave entrance camp. STASH remains viewable but forging is disabled. Even after an item is withdrawn and successfully secured in a later run, it remains FORGED and permanently ineligible for another attempt.
6. No currency or item changes occur if browser storage refuses writes, another tab modifies the stash first, a common item is selected, the item is already forged, there are zero spendable Embers, or the Ember was secured in this same run.
7. After Warden defeat, cache/shrine/waystone exploration remains open until deliberate extraction. Confirm the two-slot, one-copy active skill limit and Attack/Spell scaling remain unchanged.
8. Test both scrolling and touch targets in 844x390 landscape and iPhone Safari.
