# M04 / PR02 — Equipped Gear and Applied Stats

## Overview

Items from the previous loot milestone are no longer decorative: collected weapons, armor, and charms can now be **equipped during an expedition**, and their base stats and rolled modifiers affect live combat.

The system intentionally distinguishes three kinds of ownership: ground drops, carried items (the 24-slot bag), and equipped items (three slots). **All three remain unsecured.** Nothing persists between expeditions yet; the stash and extraction system will implement that boundary.

## Player-facing features

- Open **BAG** and view your **Weapon**, **Armor**, and **Charm** slots.
- Every carried item has an **EQUIP** button, its total useful stats (including base stats), and a stat comparison against the item already worn in that slot.
- Equipping removes the item from the bag and moves it into its slot. Replacing a slot moves the previously worn item into the bag. There are never duplicate copies.
- **UNEQUIP** moves worn gear back to the bag, only when a bag slot is free. Swapping items works even when the bag is full.
- Stat bonuses:
  - **Attack:** increases automatic basic-attack damage beyond its 25-point base.
  - **Spell:** increases automatic Cinder Burst damage beyond its 35-point base.
  - **Armor:** subtracts flat damage from incoming enemy strikes, but a valid hit always inflicts at least 1 damage.
  - **Health:** raises maximum HP beyond its 100-point base. Equipping and unequipping preserve the amount of missing health; changing gear does not reset wounds or provide an exploitable heal.
- Weapon/armor/charm *base item levels* each confer a small bonus; magic and rare affixes stack on top.
- The cache's existing **Ember Edge +10 attack damage** bonus stacks with equipped attack stats, rather than overwriting them.
- The in-game HUD displays equipped-slot count and applied attack/spell/armor bonuses. The inventory shows detailed total equipped stats and current-vs-candidate changes.
- Inventory remains a native HTML interface with touch-sized buttons; the simulation pauses while the bag is open.

## iPhone acceptance

1. Merge after CI passes, close/reopen the existing Home Screen app, and confirm version **M04 / PR02**.
2. Defeat a Brute, **PICK UP** its gear, open **BAG**, and review its stats.
3. Tap **EQUIP**. Confirm it leaves the bag, occupies the correct slot, and changes the relevant character stat.
4. Tap **UNEQUIP**. Confirm the item returns to the bag and stats return to baseline.
5. Equip two different weapons in sequence. The second replaces the first instead of destroying or duplicating anything.
6. Collect vitality gear if available: maximum HP increases. Removing it should preserve wounds while never instantly killing the character.
7. Test automatic combat with weapon damage, Cinder Burst with spell potency, and enemy hits with armor reduction. Damage is still automatic; no manual skill buttons.
8. Clear the guarded Sealed Cache and confirm its +10 basic damage stacks with worn attack power.
9. Verify bag capacity is still 24, full-bag **UNEQUIP** is blocked, and the same-slot **EQUIP** swap remains permitted.
10. Die and **RESTART EXPEDITION** or defeat Warden then finish and start another. Both carried and **worn** equipment reset. No loot is preserved until extraction is implemented.

## Deferred

- Persistent stash, secured equipment, and extraction come later in M04.
- No level/attribute requirements or item sockets/support gems yet.
- No crafting, item identification, or vendor economy yet.
- Affix scaling and balance values are prototype numbers; keep the model flexible for itemization passes.
