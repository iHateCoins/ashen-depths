# M07 PR05 — Deeper encounters, boss identities, and reward curve

## Why
Class and passive progression now needs escalating challenges and increasingly useful equipment. These additions should make **descending after a Warden kill** genuinely harder and more rewarding without making the first floor unexpectedly punishing.

## Combat
- Depth 1 retains the **original exact plan**, the familiar 300-base-HP Warden, existing enemy attack profiles, item levels and loot odds. The old seeded ?seed=42 expectations remain valid.
- Depth 2 and other even depths introduce the **Ash Reaver Warden**: quicker pursuit, a short **0.68s** readable strike warning, smaller **53px** danger circle and shorter recovery. Lower per-hit base damage than the original brute, but more frequent opportunities to punish bad positioning.
- Depth 3 and other odd depths introduce the **Stone Sentinel Warden**: slow movement, long **1.35s** warning, very broad **93px** marked attack and higher damage. Dodging and spacing should feel different from the Ash Reaver.
- Both are still the **single Warden of their respective floor**, required for descent and the existing first-clear even-floor passive milestone. Reward selection and extraction remain deliberate; boss HP still scales by depth.
- Depth 3+ remote, Cache, and Warden chambers can include **skirmishers**: low-HP fast enemies with short locked-target windups that reward movement.
- Depth 3+ non-boss rooms can contain seeded **elites**: larger, visually distinct enemies with 1.5x baseline HP, more aggressive movement and slightly stronger attacks. Elite placement and all movement are seeded and valid in the existing procedural room geometry.
- Warden, elite and skirmisher danger markers use their **real attack profile radii and windup timing** rather than generic brute/wretch circles. Hitboxes remain deterministic and line-of-sight constrained.

## Loot / build progression
- Normal non-boss **rare chance increases by +2 percentage points per depth**, capped at +20 points. Depth 1 remains at its original 13% rare roll.
- Elites **always drop at least magic** (55% base rare chance), and their equipment is one level higher than a nonelite on the same floor. This rewards the risk of confronting them; their dropped gear remains unsecured until extraction.
- Warden loot remains guaranteed rare, with the existing +2 item levels per depth and affix tier eligibility. Existing magic/rare affix mechanics, Forge Ember odds, fixed bag size, passive-award rules, cross-class unlocks and schema-1 saves stay unchanged.
- Lower-tier existing gear is **never modified in storage**. All new loot identity derives from seed/depth/enemy ID.

## Automated acceptance
- Unit tests: Depth 1 encounter parity; legal seeded elite/skirmisher placement and uniqueness of Warden; fast Reaver vs slow Sentinel; telegraphed actual AI strikes and dodge; high-level loot determinism, rarity guarantees, and tier caps.
- Cross-browser E2E: Warden 1→2→3, distinct variant profiles, deeper monsters, elite loot without premature banking.
- Existing Warden gate, passive points, saved tree allocation, skills, extraction, stash, and old item tests remain mandatory.
- All three independent Playwright browser jobs plus TypeScript/Vitest/build must pass before approval.
