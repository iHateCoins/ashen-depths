# M02 / PR04 — Dodgeable enemy attacks and the Ash Brute

## Player-facing changes

- Normal **Ash Wretches** now pause and paint a warning circle for **0.6 seconds** before striking, for **8 damage**, followed by a recovery pause.
- New larger **Ash Brutes** (220 HP) appear farther east, with slower movement, a distinct purple shape, **1.05-second** pink warning circle, a larger area and a **22-damage** strike.
- Strike positions are fixed at the start of the windup. **Tap/click outside the marked circle to dodge**. The player never automatically chases or dodges.
- Windups cancel on enemy death or disengagement. Walls block both pursuit and strikes.
- Strike impact gives hit or DODGED feedback; hit effects shake the camera.
- All gameplay remains controlled through tap/click movement while basic attacks and Cinder Burst trigger automatically.

## Acceptance checklist

1. Move toward a small enemy: a reddish warning circle appears *before* any health is lost.
2. Immediately tap outside the circle; the impact should show **DODGED** with no damage.
3. Stay within the circle: health decreases by 8 on impact, after the warning.
4. Move east toward a bigger pink Brute. Its larger warning circle takes longer to fill and inflicts 22 damage.
5. Fight using automatic basic attacks and Cinder Burst. Killing an enemy during its warning should cancel its attack.
6. Test both portrait and landscape, restart after defeat, and verify the screen remains responsive.
7. Verify CI typechecks, unit tests, production build and desktop/iPhone-compatible browser checks.

## Design boundaries

This is the first readable attack-telegraph pass, not the final animation/art pass. Crowd separation, hostile projectiles, hit sounds, enemy loot and varied elite behavior are scheduled for later milestones. Attack ranges and timings are tuning values, not final balance.
