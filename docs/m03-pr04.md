# M03 / PR04 — Optional Chamber Objectives

## Gameplay changes

This PR gives two side paths a purpose before the proper M04 loot/equipment system arrives.

**Sealed Cache**
- The generated cache chamber contains the existing enemy group assigned to that room.
- Approach the cache's center to see a small interaction prompt.
- **Opening remains locked while its original guardians survive**, even when they chase you into connected passages. The player can leave them alive, skip the entire room, or return later.
- Defeat those enemies and tap **OPEN CACHE** to gain **Ember Edge (+10 automatic basic-attack damage)** for the remainder of the expedition.
- The cache is single-use and never creates permanent equipment or stash items.

**Sanctuary Shrine**
- Spawns in an ordinary, relatively nearby chamber, chosen deterministically using the seed.
- Reach the glowing shrine and tap **USE SHRINE** to heal **up to 35 HP**.
- At full health the prompt reads **HEALTH FULL**, and the shrine is not consumed. The player can return later when hurt.
- The shrine is single-use during the expedition.

**General**
- Both interactions are optional, proximity-checked and chamber-gated (no through-wall or remote claims).
- Rewards are **run-scoped** and reset when you press New Expedition or Restart Expedition.
- Fog of war hides undiscovered shrines and cache indicators. Once visited, the minimap distinguishes these objectives and dims claimed markers.
- The native touch-friendly interaction panel works independently of Phaser camera transforms, which previously caused mobile restart-button problems.
- HUD shows side-objective progress and current basic attack bonus.
- Existing room encounters, Warden victory, optional bypassing, click/tap movement, Cinder Burst, enemy telegraphs and the Home Screen update system remain unchanged.

## iPhone acceptance

1. After approval and merge, reopen the installed iOS web app and verify **M03 / PR04** is showing.
2. Explore an ordinary chamber and find the pale-green **Sanctuary Shrine**. At 100 HP, it should refuse use without consuming the charge.
3. After taking damage, return to the shrine and tap **USE SHRINE**; HP rises by up to 35, and the shrine cannot be used again this expedition.
4. Find **Sealed Cache**; the interaction button is disabled until its originally assigned enemies have been killed. You can leave them for later.
5. Clear the cache's enemies, approach and tap **OPEN CACHE**. Confirm the status shows **Basic +10** and regular automatic hits now deal 35 instead of 25.
6. Defeat the Warden without using either side objective (optional clears should never be mandatory).
7. Start a new expedition and confirm side objectives and their benefits reset.
8. Check that the controls work in landscape, the minimap does not accidentally move the character when tapped, and version updates remain automatic.

## Explicit boundaries

Rewards in PR04 are deliberately **temporary gameplay boons**, not pretend loot. Items, rarity, equipment and inventory will be modeled in M04. No permanent account-level save is added here.
