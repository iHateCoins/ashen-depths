# M05 PR04 — Attack/Spell Classification and Physical Build Path

## Rules
Every automatic active skill now explicitly declares a damage type: **ATTACK** or **SPELL**. Attack-tagged skills add equipment Attack Power (weapon bases and Sharp affixes); spell-tagged skills add equipment Spell Power (charms and Ember affixes). Supports continue to multiply the corresponding supported damage after flat equipment scaling. Basic auto-attacks remain attacks, and the Sealed Cache's +10 Ember Edge basic-attack bonus remains basic-only, not a multiplier to all active attack skills.

## New skill
**Crescent Cleave** — ATTACK, 45 base damage, 90px impact radius, 165px targeting range, 3.6s cooldown. Auto-targets using the same line-of-sight rules as the existing skills. Has distinct golden hit visuals, supports, and takes up one of the two unique active slots. It must be unlocked through a Cache/Warden discovery and successfully extracted for use in future expeditions.

Existing Cinder Burst, Frost Bloom and Ash Lance are explicitly SPELL skills (existing damage behavior unchanged).

## Presentation
- SKILLS lists each type and which equipment stat scales it.
- Discovery rewards identify ATTACK or SPELL and the applicable stat before the player chooses.
- Combat cooldown HUD prefixes each equipped skill with its type.
- Existing saved gear and acquired skill unlocks remain valid. Stash validator accepts the newly available sixth unlock.

## Review acceptance
1. Equip Attack Power gear and confirm Crescent Cleave's displayed and dealt damage increases while Cinder Burst does not.
2. Equip Spell Power gear and confirm spell damage increases while Crescent Cleave does not.
3. Verify supports apply multiplicatively after category bonus, and AoE respects walls.
4. Secure Crescent Cleave from Cache or Warden, restart, and confirm unlocked at camp.
5. Confirm old five-unlock save loads normally; six unlocks can be secured.
6. Re-test iPhone portrait/landscape, Warden/Cache after-boss continuation, and stash integrity.

This PR is deliberately scoped to building the physical/magical progression foundation. Further reward rarity, more skills and special interactions remain subsequent milestones.
