# M02 / PR02 — Enemy pursuit and player health

## Scope
- Enemies acquire the player within 290 world units and disengage beyond 520.
- Enemies pursue via existing A* navigation, periodically recalculating routes.
- Enemies stop at contact range and deal 8 damage on a 1.1-second cooldown.
- Player has 100 HP, visible HUD, and a defeat/restart interaction.
- Dead enemies cannot pursue or attack; player auto-attacks remain unchanged.

## Manual acceptance
1. Approach an enemy; it should chase you without walking through pillars.
2. Run away; the enemy should eventually disengage.
3. Allow an enemy to approach: player HP should decrease.
4. Defeat all nearby enemies through automatic attacks.
5. Allow HP to reach zero: movement and attacks stop; tap/click RESTART to reset the scene.
6. Test in mobile Safari landscape.

## Limitations
Enemy sprites are placeholders. Enemy separation, richer AI, telegraphed attacks, loot, and death/extraction economy are deferred.
