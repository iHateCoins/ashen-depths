# M04 / PR05 — Readable Loot and Mobile Item Selection

This update is a targeted cleanup based on the owner's M04/PR04 iPhone screenshot. It preserves the full eight-room dungeon, automatic attacks, cache/shrine, multiple extraction waystones, persistent stash, equipment bonuses and death-risk rules.

## Why this PR exists

Enemies were leaving long, overlapping item names on the floor. Those text labels could obscure the player, monsters, combat markers, cache text and waystone labels. The old proximity interaction also always selected the nearest item, which was frustrating if several items overlapped.

## New gameplay

- Ground drops show **small rarity-colored icons** instead of permanent full item names. The item remains discoverable through the nearby-loot panel.
- Drops in a tight cluster are drawn as **one icon with ×N**. Gold takes precedence over blue over gray when choosing the group's visual rarity.
- The UI grouping is **presentation-only**. Every item still has its independent ID, original world position, rarity, item-level, modifiers and stored/pickup ownership.
- Approaching a single item continues to show a **PICK UP** button exactly as before.
- Approaching two or more items shows **VIEW N ITEMS** in addition to the existing quick PICK UP. The player can tap it to open a native, scrollable nearby-loot picker.
- The picker lists **each unique item**, its actual bonuses and how wearing it would change the player's current stats. Tap **TAKE** for a particular item. Its original pickup-distance, line-of-sight, and bag capacity checks still apply.
- The picker **pauses combat** like the equipment bag does, to make it usable during touch exploration. Closing the picker does not collect or remove any loot.
- The picker updates its item counts after each collection. When the final item has been taken, it closes, and the grouped world icon disappears.
- The guarded-cache area keeps its existing prominent title, but its duplicate 'SEALED · GUARDED' label is removed. Waystones keep their cyan world icons and their detailed proximity prompt, without the redundant permanently floating 'WAYSTONE · EXTRACTION' text.

## iPhone acceptance

1. Approve and merge after CI passes, then reopen the installed app and verify **M04 / PR05**.
2. Defeat a single Brute. Its item should appear as an icon without a long name sprawled across the room; approach and use **PICK UP** as before.
3. Defeat two or more enemies in roughly the same spot. A **×N** rarity-colored pile icon replaces multiple overlapping names.
4. Approach the pile. Use **VIEW N ITEMS** to inspect exact items, bonus stats and equipped-gear comparisons. Tap **TAKE** on a chosen item, not only the nearest one.
5. Continue collecting. Verify the pile count reduces, items go into the bag individually, and the picker disappears after the last one.
6. Close the picker without taking anything. All drops must remain on the floor.
7. Fill the 24-slot bag, return to a pile, and confirm taking more gear is disabled and no gear vanishes.
8. Check that the guarded cache, shrine, Warden, extraction waystones, minimap fog, native touch controls, death/restart and persistent storage all work as in previous releases.

## Deliberate non-goals

- No changes to loot probabilities, rarity rolls, damage, inventory capacity or permanent save formats.
- No automatic pickup or auto-equipping.
- No redesign of the full visual art assets in this narrow UI pass.
- No breaking migration of the existing `ashen-depths-stash-v1` local-storage records.

## Subsequent work

After this cleanup, expand character progression and expedition rewards; then consider stash backup/export and an item economy. Those systems should build on the now functional loot/extract/stash loop.
