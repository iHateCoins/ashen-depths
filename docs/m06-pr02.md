# M06 PR02 — Meaningful Equipment Base Families

## Design pillar: item-driven character progression

M06 PR01 made randomized *affixes* matter. This increment gives the item **base itself** a distinct mechanical identity. Players should make interesting equipment decisions even when comparing two common items or two rares with similar affixes.

## New equipment foundations
- Each slot retains its **four existing named starter bases**, now with distinct natural stat specialties. Examples: an Ashwood Staff sacrifices basic Attack Power to supply Spell Power; a Spiked Mace adds protection; a Traveler Coat trades armor for health; a Bone Pendant trades spell power for health.
- **Two additional advanced bases per slot** become possible from level-8+ gear (currently the Warden): Runeblade, Siegebreaker, Warden Carapace, Runesilk Robes, Crown of Cinders and Vanguard Seal.
- Base stats scale with item level plus a bounded, identity-specific offset. Both **common and affixed** gear gain from base choice.
- Each freshly generated item snapshots the exact base bonuses as `baseStats: { attack, spell, armor, health }`. This snapshot affects actual Attack/Spell auto-skill scaling, armor mitigation and maximum HP.
- The inventory, equipment loadout, nearby item picker and secured stash show `Base · +X Attack · +Y Spell …` independently of randomized affixes, as well as current totals and equip-replacement differences.

## Preservation and safety
- **No existing item gets retroactively rebalanced.** Pre-M06 PR02 gear lacks `baseStats` and continues using the original slot+item-level formula, exactly as before.
- Same schema-1 stash key. Optional snapshot values are bounded and validated (four finite non-negative integer stats); missing values are allowed for legacy gear. Corrupt values never trigger an overwrite.
- Stored snapshots are deep-copied, so a UI/client mutation of retrieved item data cannot bypass an atomic stash save.
- Withdrawing, wearing, dropping, securing and forging equipment retain the snapped base bonuses. **Forge Ember behavior is unchanged:** 25% affix erasure, 20% curse, 55% reroll, once per item, delayed until a subsequent run.
- Base choice and all affix rolls remain deterministic from dungeon seed and enemy ID. Starter enemies cannot roll Warden-only base families.
- No new gear slot, bag capacity change, drop-rate adjustment, extraction bypass or saved-state migration.

## Review acceptance
1. Compare a Rustbound Sword, Ashwood Staff, Ironmail Vest and Traveler Coat of the same level: base bonuses visibly favor different builds.
2. Collect Warden equipment and inspect a level-8 advanced base where generated. Confirm it can have both distinct base bonuses and hybrid affixes.
3. Equip attack-specialized and spell-specialized bases to verify actual basic, Attack skill and Spell skill damage changes.
4. Complete an expedition, restart, withdraw, and resecure a new base item. It must retain the exact same base stats.
5. Open a pre-PR02 stash: old items and forged items must retain their previous power and Forge Ember balances.
6. Test skill/stash/loot scroll and readability in mobile landscape and iPhone Safari.

## Automated verification
- Unit tests: base catalog, starter/advanced level gating, deterministic drops, identical-level build identities, old-slot formula, stash save/reload, deep-copy mutation protection, forge retention and malformed snapshot rejection.
- Playwright: desktop, mobile landscape and Safari stash displays with a mixture of advanced base snapshots and old legacy gear.
- Existing extraction, stash, Warden, Forge Ember, item tier, skill and browser tests remain mandatory.
