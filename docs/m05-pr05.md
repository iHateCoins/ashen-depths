# M05 PR05 — Distinct Active Combat Patterns

This milestone adds two earnable active skills whose targeting is mechanically different from ordinary center-radius AoE. Every skill still auto-targets with no buttons, respects the 2-slot loadout limit, and retains its ATTACK/SPELL equipment scaling and support trade-offs.

## New skills

| Skill | Type | Base damage | Targeting | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Piercing Volley | ATTACK | 38 | 280px attack range; 28px half-width straight projectile ray hitting all aligned enemies with unobstructed player line of sight | 3.8 seconds |
| Arc Relay | SPELL | 45 | Primary foe in 270px; jumps to up to 2 more distinct foes within 155px of the prior target; every hop checks wall LOS; second/third hits deal 80%/60% | 5.2 seconds |

Both are extraction-secured discoveries from the existing Sealed Cache or Warden. They must be permanently unlocked before they can be slotted at camp; no new save schema or separate currency.

## Rule clarity

The SKILLS menu and discovery reward text now describe line half-width and pierce, or jump range and maximum chain size, instead of calling everything an area-radius blast. Attack Power improves Piercing Volley; Spell Power improves Arc Relay. Supports adjust the real attack width or lightning jump distance via the existing radius modifier, as well as damage or cooldown as appropriate. Single-target casts still show a visible impact. New piercing attacks use golden beam effects; chains use blue arcs between the actual selected targets.

All legacy area skills retain their prior radius logic, tie-breaking, damage and cooldowns. Line and chain target search prefers the candidate that can hit the most foes, then the closest candidate, then stable ID. A wall never allows piercing through it or a chain jump through it. Each target takes at most one hit per cast.

## Persistence safety

Old 0–6 unlock saves continue to parse. The stash now derives its maximum validated unlock count from the finite skill/support catalog, so acquiring both new skills (up to 8 distinct unlocks) is legal without overwriting player equipment or inventing new storage keys.

## Player verification

1. In a new expedition, open SKILLS and inspect both locked skills and their type/pattern descriptors.
2. Clear a Sealed Cache or Warden, choose Piercing Volley or Arc Relay, and extract successfully. The next expedition can equip the secured discovery.
3. Test Piercing Volley against enemies in a straight line and offset enemies; only enemies inside the line width and in sight are struck.
4. Test Arc Relay against three neighboring enemies, including a group separated by a wall; jumps travel only among reachable foes and damage falls after the first.
5. Equip +Attack gear: physical piercing damage increases, Arc Relay does not. Equip +Spell gear for the converse.
6. Try Expansive Blast, Focused Power, or Swift Casting: adjusted widths, jump distance, damage and cooldown must match previews.
7. Inspect mobile portrait / landscape support and SKILLS menus, then finish or extract and confirm previously saved stash items are intact.
