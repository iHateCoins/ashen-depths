# M04 / PR03 — Secured Stash and Portrait HUD Cleanup

This is the first persistent progression bridge between one Ashen Depths expedition and the next. It **does not yet provide early extraction**. Instead, defeating the Warden and pressing FINISH EXPEDITION is the first successful exit, storing the real items carried and worn at completion.

## The new gameplay loop

1. Enter a dungeon and collect common, magic and rare equipment. Items in the **bag or equipped slots are still unsecured**.
2. Defeat the Warden and collect the guaranteed rare drop before finishing. Ground drops that you do not pick up are **not secured**.
3. Tap **FINISH EXPEDITION**. The game writes all carried and equipped items to an on-device, versioned stash. Only if storage confirms success will the victory screen appear.
4. The victory screen explicitly reports how many items were secured. Starting a new expedition clears the risky bag and loadout as before; **saved stash items remain**.
5. Tap the **STASH** button to view saved items from previous successful expeditions.
6. At the **entrance, before departing**, tap **WITHDRAW** on a secured item to move it into the risky 24-slot bag. Wear it using the existing BAG / EQUIP controls. This immediately removes the item from the secured stash.
7. Leaving the entrance (or killing an enemy) closes the preparation window for the rest of that run. The STASH button remains available to inspect the collection, but withdrawal is disabled until the next expedition.
8. If you die or abandon a run, withdrawn items are lost along with newly collected and equipped gear. Your other saved stash items remain intact.

## Persistence boundaries

- A small **localStorage** record under `ashen-depths-stash-v1` stores an explicit schema version, revision number, success count and up to 500 item records.
- Successful extraction is one durable write. A failed write **does not claim success or erase the running inventory**; it displays an error and allows you to retry.
- Existing malformed or future-version saves are treated as read-only errors rather than being silently wiped or overwritten.
- Duplicate source loot IDs from fixed `?seed=` replay are given new secured identities each time.
- A stale second tab cannot silently overwrite a newer stash revision.
- Withdrawing an item writes the removal to storage **before** putting the item in the new expedition bag, avoiding duplication.
- Stash data is **local to this browser/app installation**, not synchronized to other phones, Safari profiles or your GitHub account. Clearing website data can erase it. There is no online account or backend.
- Because active expeditions are not saved yet, **refreshing or closing mid-run currently abandons unsecured items**, including items withdrawn during camp preparation. Withdraw only gear you're willing to risk.

## Screenshot-driven HUD pass

The tall iPhone screenshot of M04/PR02 showed a long debug status line running under the minimap and the FINISH EXPEDITION button competing with top controls.

- Hide the dense status/position text in normal play (still accessible through developer debug mode).
- Keep HP and a compact objective summary on the left, with word-wrap constrained to stay away from the top-right minimap.
- Hide the DEBUG toggle on narrow displays; F3 debug remains available with a keyboard.
- Place FINISH EXPEDITION near the bottom center so it no longer covers the title, HP or minimap.
- Keep version/seed and Cinder Burst readouts at the bottom, with stash and bag buttons next to each other.

## Acceptance checklist (iPhone Home Screen web app)

1. Merge only after CI passes. Reopen the existing Home Screen app and confirm it updates to **M04 / PR03**.
2. Verify top-left health/objective text doesn't cross into the top-right minimap, DEBUG no longer obstructs the map, and FINISH is away from the top HUD.
3. Kill a Brute, collect its equipment, **EQUIP** it, then defeat the Warden and collect its rare drop.
4. Tap **FINISH EXPEDITION**. The victory screen should say **2 items secured** (one worn and one carried).
5. Start another expedition. The bag and equipment reset, but **STASH 2** should remain; open it to inspect both saved items.
6. Withdraw one item at the entrance. The stash count falls and bag count increases; equip it.
7. Walk away and reopen STASH. Withdraw controls should be disabled for the remainder of the run.
8. Lose/restart or reload. Previously secured items still exist, but the withdrawn item is gone because it was at risk.
9. Complete another expedition using the same `?seed=` URL. Saved identities must remain unique.
10. Existing fog, minimap, cache, shrine, combat skills and touch interactions continue working.
