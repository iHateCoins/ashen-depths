# M05 / PR03 — Extraction-Secured Skill Discovery

## Gameplay
- Existing players keep secured gear and completed expeditions. The schema-1 stash now accepts an optional `unlocks` array; old valid saves without it load unchanged.
- New players start with **Cinder Burst** only. Frost Bloom, Ash Lance and the three supports are locked until earned.
- **Open the guarded Sealed Cache:** retain the existing +10 basic damage reward and offer **one optional discovery choice**.
- **Defeat the Warden:** retain rare gear and Finish Expedition; offer another discovery choice. Cache and Warden choices can be claimed in either order.
- Each discovery offers one still-unknown skill/support. Choices are **pending**, not usable immediately.
- The DISCOVERIES button opens a mobile dialog, pauses combat and remains available until the choice is made or the expedition ends. Ignore or close it to keep exploring; unused offers are forfeited on extraction.
- **Successfully extract through a waystone or Finish Expedition** to secure gear plus pending discoveries in **the same atomic existing localStorage transaction**. A failed save never awards unlocks and leaves the run retryable.
- **Death, page reload or Restart** before successful extraction loses pending choices, as with carried gear.
- Secured skills/supports are available in the camp SKILLS menu in **later expeditions**; they do not increase the two-skill limit or bypass exclusive support assignment.
- Duplicate or invalid unlock IDs are rejected before writing. Invalid existing stash remains untouched, and stale tabs cannot overwrite recently saved discoveries.

## Manual acceptance
1. New playthrough: Frost Bloom, Ash Lance and supports are LOCKED; Cinder Burst available.
2. Clear cache defenders and OPEN CACHE; collect the +10 attack boon and see DISCOVERIES 1.
3. Choose Frost Bloom. It appears as one *unsecured* discovery; it does not become immediately equippable.
4. Escape at a waystone. New Expedition now allows Frost Bloom at camp, and previous stash items remain intact.
5. In another run, defeat the Warden and choose a support. Finish Expedition to secure it; return to SKILLS and equip the support in a subsequent expedition.
6. Repeat the Warden choice but die/restart before extraction. The pending reward must be lost.
7. Simulate storage failure: exit remains unfinalized and pending unlocks remain at risk until a successful retry.
8. Recheck post-Warden cache/shrine/waystone interaction, loot, equipment, mobile dock and updater.
