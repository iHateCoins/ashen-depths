# M04 / PR04 — Early Waystone Extraction

## The goal

M04 now supports a meaningful loot-extraction choice: **retreat early and save your expedition gear, or push deeper and risk losing it to claim better drops**. The existing Warden victory still works exactly as before.

## World design

- Each generated dungeon contains **two deterministic extraction waystones** in separate ordinary chambers.
- The near and distant exits are chosen for different routes. Neither overlaps the entrance preparation camp, Sanctuary Shrine, guarded Sealed Cache or Warden chamber.
- Cyan glowing waystones are visible in the world under the normal fog-of-war. **Only discovered waystones** appear as cyan dots on the minimap; undiscovered rooms remain hidden.
- No additional corridors or dungeon scene transitions. You can explore, backtrack and revisit branches as before.
- Rooms are **never required to be cleared** to use an exit. You can retreat while dangerous monsters are alive, if they do not hit you during escape.

## Escape behavior

1. Find a cyan waystone in a side chamber and approach its center.
2. When near the waystone, an **EXTRACT NOW** button appears. Entering the room does **not** automatically extract.
3. Tap EXTRACT NOW to begin a **2.5-second channel**. Your character stops any queued movement but existing monsters keep attacking. Automatic attacks and skills also continue while channeling.
4. Taking damage, stepping outside the interaction radius, tapping CANCEL ESCAPE, clicking a new movement destination, or opening the bag/stash interrupts the channel and resets the timer. Escaping from within enemy attack range is therefore risky.
5. Complete the uninterrupted channel to secure **all carried and worn equipment**, using the same versioned on-device stash transaction as the Warden finish.
6. The game only shows **ESCAPED ALIVE** after a successful save. Tap **NEW EXPEDITION** to return to camp; your secure stash is preserved and the active bag/gear resets.
7. If the device refuses the stash write, an error is shown and **the current run and all its gear remain available**. The player may attempt extraction again when storage is working. The game must never silently report successful extraction or erase inventory after a failed save.

## Warden alternative

You may ignore both waystones, defeat the Warden and pick up its guaranteed rare item, then use FINISH EXPEDITION to secure the run as in PR03. The Warden outcome still displays its separate **WARDEN DEFEATED** victory screen. Both outcomes add one successful expedition to the same stash progress counter.

## Test on iPhone

1. After owner approval and successful Pages deployment, launch the Home Screen app and verify **M04 / PR04**.
2. Explore until you spot a cyan waystone; check the minimap only marks it once its chamber has been discovered.
3. Step close to the waystone but **don't press EXTRACT NOW**. Standing beside it must not save items or end the run.
4. Tap EXTRACT NOW, then CANCEL ESCAPE. Confirm the game stays playable.
5. Start again, move away or take a hit while channeling. The countdown should reset and the extraction must not happen.
6. Collect and equip some gear, then complete an uninterrupted escape. Confirm **ESCAPED ALIVE** and the correct secured-item count.
7. Start a new expedition. Withdraw your saved items from the STASH at the entrance, just as in PR03.
8. Start another run, ignore both waystones, defeat Warden, collect the rare item and FINISH EXPEDITION. Both methods must preserve gear without duplicates.
9. Verify touch interaction in both portrait and landscape and make sure the new panel stays clear of BAG, STASH and the minimap.

## Deferred

- Extraction quality tiers, escape consumables and danger modifiers.
- A richer map legend/waypoint overlay.
- Partial item retention on death and insurance.
- Browser backup/export/import for locally secured loot.
- Improved spacing/grouping for overlapping ground-loot labels, noted in the PR03 gameplay screenshot.
