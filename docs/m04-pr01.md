# M04 / PR01 — Unsecured Loot and Expedition Bag

## Purpose

This is the first **real loot** implementation for Ashen Depths. It introduces deterministic enemy drops, item rarity, base equipment categories, structured modifiers, visible ground pickups, and a limited expedition bag. **Extraction, equipment slots, stash and permanent persistence are deliberately not implemented yet.**

## Player-facing behavior

- Defeated enemies can drop an item where they died. Wretches have a 60% chance to drop something, Brutes always drop, and the Warden is guaranteed a rare item.
- Drops roll **common / magic / rare** rarity, a base item (weapon, armor or charm), item level, and 0 / 1 / 2 numeric affixes. These affixes are **item data, not yet equipped combat bonuses**.
- Ground drops persist in the current dungeon until picked up or the run ends. They are visibly colored by rarity but remain hidden behind exploration fog until revealed.
- Walk or tap near an item to show its name, rarity and affixes; tap **PICK UP** to place it in the expedition bag. Movement and combat never automatically loot.
- Tap **BAG** at the lower right to inspect item details, rarity, level and modifiers.
- Bag capacity is **24 equipment items**. A full bag blocks pickup without destroying the ground item. Open the bag and tap **DROP** on any item to free a slot; discarded gear stays on the ground.
- The bag overlay pauses the prototype's gameplay while you inspect/discard, so iPhone interactions do not expose you to unpredictable damage.
- Item names, affixes and drop chance are seeded from the dungeon seed and enemy ID, not elapsed time or device state.
- **Everything is unsecured** in this PR. Starting a new expedition or dying wipes both carried and uncollected ground gear. Do not expect collected loot to survive the Warden victory screen yet.

## Manual acceptance

1. After merge/deployment, launch the installed iPhone Home Screen app and verify **M04 / PR01**.
2. Fight nearby enemies. Look for white, blue and gold equipment drops; Brutes always drop something.
3. Approach one: its proximity prompt appears. Tap **PICK UP**. The bag count should increase.
4. Tap **BAG**. Inspect the item details and stat modifiers (not yet active bonuses). Tap **DROP** and close the bag; the item should reappear for another pickup.
5. Collect gear until the 24-slot cap (may require multiple enemies). A full bag leaves new ground loot untouched.
6. Try moving away from a drop: the pickup prompt disappears and items cannot be collected remotely.
7. Die and press **RESTART EXPEDITION**, or finish the Warden and press **NEW EXPEDITION**. Loot does **not** persist across either outcome yet.
8. Confirm fog/minimap, touch movement, optional cache/shrine, automatic combat and iPhone build auto-update remain intact.
