# M04 / PR06 — Shared Contextual Interaction Dock

## Screenshot-driven correction

The owner tested M04/PR05 and found **EXTRACT NOW** could physically cover nearby loot controls when dropped equipment appeared beside an extraction waystone. The same failure can occur with the Sealed Cache or a shrine in a room containing gear.

This update resolves the collision at the component layout level rather than simply moving one floating window to another fixed coordinate.

## Player experience

- The **Loot**, **Objective** (cache/shrine) and **Extract** panels now share one bottom-centered, safe-area-aware dock.
- **One contextual action nearby:** no extra UI or tabs. Its existing button appears directly.
- **Two or three actions nearby:** large clearly labeled **LOOT**, **OBJECTIVE** and/or **EXTRACT** tabs appear. Exactly **one context panel** is visible at a time. Each remains selectable even while another activity is nearby.
- The default when more than one action first becomes available is **LOOT**. Players can inspect and collect dropped equipment even if they're standing directly on a waystone.
- Tap **EXTRACT** to reveal the waystone and start a deliberate 2.5-second channel. During the channel, the extraction panel becomes active and **CANCEL ESCAPE** remains accessible.
- Tap **LOOT** or **OBJECTIVE** during a channel to **cancel extraction** before switching to the selected interaction. This prevents a surprising loss of items from a hidden escape countdown.
- When the final ground item is taken, LOOT disappears as an option and any still-available waystone or objective becomes directly accessible without an extra tab.
- The dock is constrained above the existing **STASH** / **BAG** buttons and honors the iOS safe-area inset in portrait and landscape.
- All existing accessibility labels, HTML controls, item IDs, range checks, line-of-sight checks, equipment, loot pickup validation and atomic stash persistence remain unchanged.
- The large full-screen inventory, nearby-item picker, victory, death and stash views still behave as modals above the dock.

## Acceptance on installed iPhone app

1. After owner approval and deployment, reopen Ashen Depths and verify **M04 / PR06**.
2. Approach a waystone with **no loot nearby**: the normal **EXTRACT NOW** panel appears, with no unnecessary tabs.
3. Defeat a Brute right next to that same waystone. The dock offers **LOOT / EXTRACT** instead of piling two windows over one another.
4. With **LOOT** selected, inspect its item and tap **PICK UP**. The **EXTRACT** view becomes directly available when no loot remains.
5. Repeat with several close drops, use **VIEW N ITEMS**, select the gear you want, and return to the dock.
6. Start extracting while loot is still nearby, then tap **LOOT**. The escape channel must cancel; the items remain on the ground and no stash write occurs.
7. Repeat near a shrine or Sealed Cache, where **LOOT / OBJECTIVE** should both remain selectable.
8. Verify the dock never touches the **STASH** or **BAG** buttons or extends off the visible screen in portrait and landscape.
9. Recheck normal early extraction, Warden finish, persistent stash/withdrawal, automatic combat, restart and installed-web-app updates.

## Regression coverage

The browser suite explicitly creates real deterministic Brute items at a generated waystone and shrine, then uses actual desktop/mobile/WebKit buttons and bounding-box checks to verify interaction exclusivity, loot selection, channel interruption and durable extraction. No seed files, saved item data or gameplay tuning are changed.
