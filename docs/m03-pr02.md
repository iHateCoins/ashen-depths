# M03 / PR02 — Exploration, Fog of War, and Room Discovery

## Changes

- Track **visited chambers** independently of the seeded dungeon generator. Discovering a room requires **entering the room**, not merely seeing it from a corridor.
- Reveal only nearby walkable tiles within seven 32px cells, with line-of-sight blocked by solid rock/walls. Previously seen floor stays visible **for the current expedition**.
- Draw dark fog over unexplored dungeon floor. Enemy sprites, combat effects, and secret landmarks beyond explored terrain stay hidden.
- Add a **screen-fixed minimap**, accessible in iPhone landscape, with player location, mapped floor, and icons for **previously discovered** chamber landmarks.
- Show a compact **Explored X / 8 Rooms** progress count. Display a one-time discovery popup when entering ordinary, cache, or deep chambers.
- Prevent tapping on the minimap from issuing accidental movement commands.
- Clear discovery and fog state when a trial is replayed or a run restarts, even if the player repeats the same `?seed=` layout.
- Preserve connected navigation, click/tap movement, automatic combat, Cinder Burst, enemy windup telegraphs, combat-trial victory/restart, and iOS web-app version checking.

## Manual acceptance

1. After merging, close and reopen the **existing Home Screen app**. It should auto-update to **M03 / PR02**.
2. Observe the minimap at the upper right. Initially only the entrance area and nearby tiles should appear.
3. Walk from the entrance into hallways and neighboring rooms. Newly explored passages fill in and the counter increases only on **entry** to each room.
4. Navigate toward the cache or deep chamber. The appropriate marker should only appear on the minimap after that room has been entered.
5. Walk back to the entrance. Previously explored tiles stay mapped.
6. Try tapping the minimap. It should *not* send a movement command.
7. Clear three waves and press **Replay Trial**, or lose and press **Restart Expedition**. Fog and discovery should reset to the entrance.
8. Open `?seed=42` in Safari. The dungeon remains reproducible while each new run starts with an unexplored map.

## Explicit non-goals

- This pass implements **run-only exploration progress**, not persistent discoveries between expeditions.
- The map does not support tap-to-navigate, quest markers, dynamic enemy detection, or a larger fullscreen overlay yet.
- Floor tiles remain mapped after leaving line of sight; this is *exploration fog*, not a real-time sight system for hostile actors.
- The sealed cache and deep chamber are **landmarks**, not yet loot/extraction mechanics.
- Combat remains the existing three-wave training encounter; **room-specific encounters** will follow in the next M03 PR.
