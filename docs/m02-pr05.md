# M02 / PR05 — Repeatable Combat Trial

## What changed
- Replaced eight fixed practice enemies with a repeatable **three-wave encounter**.
- 1.5-second opening countdown, then waves of 3 / 5 / 7 enemies, progressively adding Ash Brutes.
- Three-second breather after clearing a wave, with up to 20 HP recovered (never above maximum).
- Reachable, deterministic enemy spawn planning using the navigation grid and A*; never spawn in blocked cells or on top of the player.
- Wave number, remaining enemies, and countdown status are visible in the HUD.
- Clearing the third wave displays **TRIAL CLEARED** with a touch-friendly **REPLAY TRIAL** option; defeat retains **RESTART EXPEDITION**.
- Encounter state, enemies, combat cooldowns, health, kill count and UI reset on restart.
- The app's previously validated iOS automatic update system is unchanged.

## Manual verification
1. Open the game in desktop Safari/Chrome or the installed iPhone Home Screen app after merging. Confirm the title reads **M02 / PR05**.
2. A short countdown precedes three Ash Wretches. Dodge red warning areas while automatically attacking.
3. Defeat the wave; confirm the intermission and bounded +20 HP recovery.
4. Wave 2 introduces an Ash Brute; wave 3 contains two Brutes.
5. Clearing wave 3 displays a victory panel; **REPLAY TRIAL** should reset progression and enemy spawns.
6. If defeated, **RESTART EXPEDITION** should reset everything and begin again at wave 1.
7. Confirm the build label changed in the existing Home Screen app without re-adding the shortcut.

## Deferred
Finite training trial is not yet the branching dungeon, extraction economy, loot, leveling or persistent progression. Those belong to later milestones. Final spawn distributions, difficulty tuning, environment, enemy art, and UI are subject to future iteration.
